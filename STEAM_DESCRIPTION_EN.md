[h1]🚗 Minidoracat Vehicle Manager for B42[/h1]
[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]

[hr][/hr]

[h2]✨ What is this?[/h2]
A planned vehicle management system with vehicle binding, ownership protection, personal and faction fleet management, MiniMap tracking, and Economy rental integration.

[h2]🚧 Development status[/h2]
Only the MOD project foundation exists at this time. No in-game vehicle management features have been implemented yet.

[h2]🧰 Planned features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Vehicle binding[/b]: Register and manage personal or faction vehicles
[*] [b]Fleet tracking[/b]: Use the optional MiniMap integration to show your vehicles and vehicles owned by members of the same faction
[*] [b]Management interface[/b]: Review vehicles, ownership, permissions, and rental status in one place
[*] [b]Binding slots[/b]: Use items or Economy currency to increase the number of vehicles that can be bound
[*] [b]Rental and billing[/b]: Plan daily or monthly charges through the optional Economy integration
[*] [b]Ownership protection[/b]: Plan server-authoritative validation to reduce unauthorized use, theft, and damage
[/list]
