description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=[h2]✨ What is this[/h2]
description=Vehicle claiming and protection for multiplayer servers: claim a car to your account and other players can no longer drive it, siphon its fuel, strip its parts or tow it away. Share it with friends or your faction, and manage your cars from a fleet window and the minimap.
description=
description=[h2]🔒 Claiming and protection[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Right-click next to a car, choose "Vehicle Manager → Claim vehicle", and confirm what the protection covers
description=[*] Claims belong to your account, so you keep your cars when your character dies; the server sets how many cars each player can claim
description=[*] Players without permission cannot: open or close doors, unlock, get in, start the engine, add or siphon fuel, deflate tires, install or remove parts, repair, smash windows, tow, or install/remove AutoDrive GPS and autopilot modules
description=[*] When blocked they see "This vehicle is protected by its owner" and the car stays as it was
description=[*] Owners can unlock, open doors (without the alarm) and start the car without a physical key (servers can turn this off)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🤝 Sharing[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Share with specific players or your faction and pick each permission: ride, drive, trunk, fuel, install/repair, remove parts, tow, see location
description=[*] Private by default; nothing is shared automatically
description=[*] Only the owner can rename, share, unclaim or transfer
description=[*] If the faction is renamed, disbanded or gets a new leader, faction sharing pauses until the owner confirms again
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🗂️ Fleet window[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Open it with the steering wheel button on the right side of the screen
description=[*] See your cars, cars shared with you and where they were last seen
description=[*] Rename, share, transfer, unclaim, or report a car lost (it is released after a waiting period)
description=[*] Admins get an extra page to deal with problem claims
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🗺️ Minimap tracking (optional)[/h2]
description=With Minidoracat MiniMap installed, your cars and shared cars you may "see location" of show on the minimap and world map. Nobody else can see them.
description=
description=[h2]⚠️ What is protected[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Normal player actions are protected; damage from weapons, zombies and crashes is not
description=[*] Someone using a cheating client may briefly get into a seat; the server notices within about a second and acts on it (admins choose between logging only or removing them from the car)
description=[*] Every decision is made on the server; clients only receive data they are allowed to see
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]🔁 Moving from Mysterious Vehicle Claim Key[/h2]
description=An admin presses "Import all claims from MVCK" once: loaded vehicles move over at once, the rest the next time they load. MVCK data is not deleted; remove MVCK yourself when you are happy. Back up the save first.
